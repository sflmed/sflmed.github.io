About this site

SFL Med Health Notes is a small patient education site from South Florida Multispecialty Medical Group (SFL Medical Group), with offices in Miami and Homestead, Florida.

The articles are short and practical. They cover everyday habits and techniques, such as checking shoe fit, watching for leg swelling, and keeping track of joint symptoms, Each article is based on guidance from government and university health sources, which are linked at the end of the post.

They are written for general education. They do not replace a visit with your own clinician and they are not a diagnosis.

To learn more about our services or meet our physicians, visit sflmedicalgroup.com.
